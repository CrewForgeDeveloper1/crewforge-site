Threads and torque

Identify a bolt with a pitch gauge, build its proof load, torque it and watch the preload. Force a cross-thread, mix a grade 8 bolt with a grade 2 nut, pull past the click and pressure-test a flange.

Start the lesson Summary sheet (PDF)
The sample lesson. Every CrewForge lesson works like this one: short simulator pages, one idea each, with the narrator, practice questions and a summary sheet.

Open full screen · 6 pages · 11 practice questions · narrated (AI voice) · exam preparation only: always check the maker's data and the plant torque spec.

What's in the lesson

  1. Thread words. A 1/2-13 UNC bolt on the bench, and the same thread magnified above it, drawn at its true pitch.
  2. Thread ID on the bench. A bolt from the parts bin, lying on the bench.
  3. Grades and proof load. A 1/2-13 grade 5 bolt through two steel plates, its head shown end-on at the left.
  4. Torque and preload. The same joint, cut in half: a 1/2-13 grade 5 bolt clamping two steel plates.
  5. The tightening pattern. An NPS 4 Class 150 flange from above: 8 × 5/8-11 bolts on a 7.5 in bolt circle.
  6. Putting it together. The job: refit a pump discharge flange (NPS 4 Class 150) after a seal change.

How it works

  • One idea per page, each built around a simulator you work yourself. The last page puts it all together on one job.
  • A narrator reads each step aloud. Mute it or slow it down at the top right, or read it yourself.
  • Each step tells you what to do and what to watch. Stuck? Hint, then Show me: the simulator does it, then hands back.
  • Practice questions along the way. Pick a wrong answer and you see why it's wrong, then get a new, similar question.
  • After the steps, free play: every control unlocked, Reset, and "Give me a challenge".
  • Nothing locks. Back and Next always work, and your place is kept in this browser.
  • Print the short summary sheet: the rules and key numbers, light on toner.
