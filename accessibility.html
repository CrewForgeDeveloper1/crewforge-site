Accessibility

Accessibility statement

Last updated November 15, 2026

We want every apprentice to be able to use CrewForge, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard, captions or zoom. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA, and we keep working toward it.

What we do

  • Pages use real headings, labelled form fields and text alternatives for images.
  • Text and buttons are sized and coloured for contrast on our dark background; pages zoom to 200 % without losing content, and work on a phone.
  • Status colours (green, yellow, red) always come with words, so colour is never the only signal.
  • Lesson narration comes with the same words on screen. You can mute it, slow it down or read at your own pace.
  • Videos have captions. They use AI narration from a written script.
  • Every simulator lesson has a printable summary sheet, and paid lessons come with a full textbook chapter.

Known limits

  • Some simulators are drawn graphics you drag or tap. Many controls also work with a keyboard, but not all of them yet, and a screen reader can't describe every moving part. The lesson text and summary sheet carry the same information.
  • Some older YouTube videos were captioned automatically and may contain errors.
  • CrewForge Learn (learn.crewforge.ca) runs on Moodle, which has its own accessibility features; some quiz types may be harder to use with assistive technology.

Ask for another format or report a barrier

If something doesn't work for you, tell us what you were trying to do and which page you were on. We'll reply within two business days and, where we can, give you the content another way (for example a text version of a simulator exercise or more time on a timed quiz). Email signup [at] crewforge [dot] ca with the subject Accessibility, or see contact us.
