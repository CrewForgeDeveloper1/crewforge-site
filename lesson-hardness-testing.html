Hardness testing

Run a file test, then step through a Rockwell C test on the bench tester. Pick the right scale, read a Brinell dent under the microscope and check a pump shaft against its drawing. Leave the ball in on a hardened shaft and see what happens.

The sample lesson. Every CrewForge lesson works like this one: short simulator pages, one idea each, with the narrator, practice questions and a summary sheet.

Open full screen · 7 pages · 13 practice questions · narrated (AI voice) · exam preparation only: always check the tester maker's manual and the drawing or spec.

What's in the lesson

  1. Hardness and the file test. A hardened tool-steel wear strip is clamped in the bench vise.
  2. Rockwell C, step by step. A bench Rockwell hardness tester.
  3. Rockwell B and picking the scale. The same tester set up for the B scale: a 1/16 in ball instead of the diamond cone, 100 kgf total.
  4. Brinell: a big ball and a microscope. A bench hydraulic Brinell tester.
  5. Valid tests and conversions. A test piece in section on the anvil, drawn to scale, with a hardness dent in its top face.
  6. What the number means. The bench Rockwell tester again, with three slices of the same 4140 bar: annealed, oil quenched, and quenched and tempered.
  7. Putting it together: check a shaft. A replacement pump shaft came in for a rebuild.

How it works

  • One idea per page, each built around a simulator you work yourself. The last page puts it all together on one job.
  • A narrator reads each step aloud. Mute it or slow it down at the top right, or read it yourself.
  • Each step tells you what to do and what to watch. Stuck? Hint, then Show me: the simulator does it, then hands back.
  • Practice questions along the way. Pick a wrong answer and you see why it's wrong, then get a new, similar question.
  • After the steps, free play: every control unlocked, Reset, and "Give me a challenge".
  • Nothing locks. Back and Next always work, and your place is kept in this browser.
  • Print the short summary sheet: the rules and key numbers, light on toner.
