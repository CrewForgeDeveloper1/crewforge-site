Load weight and centre of gravity
WHAT YOU'RE LOOKING AT
A load on 6×6 cribbing under the crane: a plate, a round bar, a pipe, a block or a skid with a motor on it. Weigh it from its size and density, find its centre of gravity, then lift it on two hoists or on one hook with a two-leg wire rope bridle and watch what each leg carries.
