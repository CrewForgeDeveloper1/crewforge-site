Certifications to get after your Red Seal millwright

Your Red Seal is the trade credential. These add-on certifications can open up predictive maintenance, shutdown and specialist work. Here is who issues each one, whether you need it, and what it takes.

As of November 2026. Each guide links to the issuing body's or regulator's own page. Rules and fees change, so check with them before you apply. Nothing here is legal advice.

Where to start

  • Most useful on any site: rigging. Know your province's rules first.
  • Best next step into reliability: vibration analyst CAT I, then balancing.
  • Hydraulics-heavy plant: IFPS Industrial Hydraulic Mechanic.
  • Lube routes and PM: ICML MLT I.
  • Fabrication and structural repairs: ask your employer about a CWB ticket.

Questions

Do I need any of these to write the Red Seal exam?

No. The Red Seal Industrial Mechanic (Millwright) exam is arranged through your provincial or territorial apprenticeship authority. None of these certifications are needed to write it.

Will CrewForge make me certified?

No. CrewForge prepares you for the Red Seal exam. Our lessons and simulators do not certify you and do not count toward any certification's training hours.

Are the fees in Canadian dollars?

Most of these bodies are based in the United States and list fees in US dollars. Each guide says which currency it uses.
