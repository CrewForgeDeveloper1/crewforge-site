Questions

FAQ

Is this a Red Seal certificate?

No. CrewForge prepares you for the Red Seal Industrial Mechanic (Millwright) exam. Your Red Seal endorsement comes from passing that exam through your provincial apprenticeship authority. Our certificate recognises completion of the CrewForge program.

Can I challenge the exam without an apprenticeship?

Many provinces let experienced workers challenge the exam once they can document enough hours in the trade. The hours and paperwork differ by province, so confirm with your apprenticeship office first. CrewForge covers the full exam content so you're ready when you're approved.

Does this replace college or in-school training?

No. CrewForge is extra help alongside it, like a tutor you can use any time. Lessons follow the same level sequence as your in-school training, so you can prepare before a block, catch up on anything you missed in class, and review before your level exams and the Red Seal. You still complete the training your province requires.

Can I join from outside Canada?

Yes, everyone is welcome. The program follows the Canadian Red Seal Industrial Mechanic (Millwright) standard, and the skills and maths apply to millwright and industrial mechanic work anywhere. If you want the Red Seal endorsement itself, you'll need to go through a Canadian provincial or territorial apprenticeship authority.

When does it launch, and what will it cost?

Level 1 early access is planned for Winter 2027, with Levels 2 to 4 following through 2027. People who join early pay less: $299 for the Full Program or $74 for Level 1, with every lesson as it's released. After that, a Level Pass is $99 per level and the Full Program is $349 for all four levels plus Red Seal Exam Prep (CAD, before tax). See pricing.

Is it based on the current exam?

Yes. Lessons and question banks are built from the 2026 Red Seal Occupational Standard and its exam weightings. We never reproduce real exam questions.
