Questions about buying

How do I get access after I pay?

You create a CrewForge Learn account at learn.crewforge.ca, confirm your email, then pay. Your course opens straight away, with every lesson already built. New lessons appear in the same course as they're released.

How long does my access last?

Level 1 access lasts one year from the day you buy. Full Program early access lasts until 12 months after the final level is released. If you need more time, extensions are $29 for 3 months or $49 for 6 months.

What devices can I use?

Any up-to-date web browser on a computer, tablet or phone. The simulators work with a mouse or a finger, and the textbook chapters print or save as PDF. Your account is for one person.

How do I pay?

Checkout is through PayPal. You can pay with a credit card, a Visa Debit or Debit Mastercard, or a PayPal account; you don't need a PayPal account to use a card. Interac-only debit cards can't be used. Prices are in Canadian dollars.

Can I get a refund?

If Level 1 isn't complete by March 31, 2027, you can ask for a full refund until April 30, 2027. We also refund duplicate or mistaken charges. See the refund policy for the rest.

Can I start with Level 1 and upgrade later?

Yes. Level 1 members can move up to the Full Program at a members' price, offered inside the Level 1 course. Upgrades replace refunds: the Level 1 payment isn't refunded when you upgrade.

Is the certificate a Red Seal credential?

No. A CrewForge certificate recognises that you finished a private study program. It is not a Red Seal endorsement, a Certificate of Qualification or a provincial credential, and it doesn't count toward apprenticeship hours. Your Red Seal comes from passing the exam through your provincial or territorial apprenticeship authority.

Is CrewForge available in New Brunswick?

No. CrewForge isn't offered in New Brunswick at this time, so we can't sell to New Brunswick residents or add them to the launch list.

Can my employer pay?

Yes. Employers can buy seats for their crew at list price, with volume quotes. See for employers or contact us.
