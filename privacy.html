Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

CrewForge respects your privacy and follows Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL). This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

What we collect

  • Launch list: your first name, email address, role and province, and the date and wording of your consent.
  • Messages you send us: what you write and your contact details.
  • Learners (once courses open): your account details, course progress, quiz and simulator results, and basic technical logs such as sign-in times and IP addresses used to keep accounts secure.
  • Website statistics: we count page views and visits on crewforge.ca with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't track you across sites and doesn't identify you; we only see totals such as which pages are popular and which countries visits come from.

Why we collect it

  • To tell you about the CrewForge launch and new training, if you asked us to.
  • To reply to your enquiry.
  • To run your courses, track your progress and issue certificates.

We don't sell your information. We don't use it for anything you haven't agreed to.

Who processes it

We use trusted service providers to send email and host our sites. Some may store information outside Canada; when they do, it's protected by contract and may be subject to the laws of that country.

How long we keep it

Launch-list details are kept until you unsubscribe. Learner records are kept while your account is active and for a reasonable time after, as needed for certificates and records, then deleted.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from any email with one click, or email us.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete your information at signup [at] crewforge [dot] ca.

YouTube API Services

CrewForge publishes free training videos on its own YouTube channel. Our internal production tool uses YouTube API Services to manage that channel only: setting video titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlists and publish times. By using our channel or any part of our services that relies on YouTube, you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

  • What the tool accesses: only CrewForge's own YouTube channel, through the channel owner's sign-in. It does not access, collect or store information about viewers, other channels or other YouTube users.
  • What we store: the IDs, titles and publish times of our own videos and playlists, kept in our production files to run our publishing schedule. No personal information is collected through YouTube API Services, and nothing is shared with or sold to anyone.
  • Deletion: stored video IDs and schedule data are deleted when the video is removed from our channel, or within 30 days of a request to signup [at] crewforge [dot] ca. Access granted to our tool can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.
