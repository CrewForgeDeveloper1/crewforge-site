Refunds

Refund policy

Last updated November 15, 2026

This policy is part of our terms of sale.

Pre-sale promise

Early access is sold before the program is finished. If Level 1 isn't complete (every Level 1 lesson released) by March 31, 2027, you can ask for a full refund of your early-access payment (Level 1 or Full Program) until April 30, 2027.

Changed your mind

If you ask within 14 days of buying and you've opened no more than two lessons, we refund you in full.

We always refund

  • Duplicate or mistaken charges.
  • A purchase made from New Brunswick, where CrewForge isn't offered (the enrolment is cancelled).
  • A course we stop offering before it's delivered.

We don't refund

  • Upgrades: Level 1 members move up to the Full Program at a members' price instead. The Level 1 payment isn't refunded when you upgrade.
  • Access extensions once they've started.
  • Unused time on an access period, after the cases above.

How to ask

Email info [at] crewforge [dot] ca from the address on your CrewForge Learn account with the subject Refund and the course name. We reply within two business days and refund to the original payment method through PayPal within 30 days. When a refund is made, the course enrolment ends.

Nothing in this policy limits any right you have under the consumer protection law of your province or territory.
