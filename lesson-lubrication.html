Lubrication

Watch the oil film carry a bearing, pick a viscosity grade, work out how much grease and how often, then keep pumping the grease gun past full and watch the bearing run hot and the seal lip lift.

Start the lesson Summary sheet (PDF)
The sample lesson. Every CrewForge lesson works like this one: short simulator pages, one idea each, with the narrator, practice questions and a summary sheet.

Open full screen · 7 pages · 13 practice questions · narrated (AI voice) · exam preparation only: always check the machine maker's lube chart and the lubricant data sheet.

What's in the lesson

  1. The oil film. A gearbox shaft bearing (a 6309 ball bearing), cut across the shaft, running in an oil bath.
  2. Viscosity grades. The same gearbox bearing.
  3. Grease and NLGI. A fan's pillow block, cut through the shaft and drawn to scale around a 6309 ball bearing, with lithium grease in the free space.
  4. How much, how often. The same pillow block, drain plug out.
  5. Over-greasing. The pillow block again, with the fill gauge for the free space and an infrared thermometer reading the bearing.
  6. Dirt and water. A helical gear reducer on its plinth, with a sight glass, a breather on top, the oil used to top it up and a filter cart.
  7. Putting it together. The job: an induced-draft fan's 6309 bearing to regrease, and the fan's gearbox to top up.

How it works

  • One idea per page, each built around a simulator you work yourself. The last page puts it all together on one job.
  • A narrator reads each step aloud. Mute it or slow it down at the top right, or read it yourself.
  • Each step tells you what to do and what to watch. Stuck? Hint, then Show me: the simulator does it, then hands back.
  • Practice questions along the way. Pick a wrong answer and you see why it's wrong, then get a new, similar question.
  • After the steps, free play: every control unlocked, Reset, and "Give me a challenge".
  • Nothing locks. Back and Next always work, and your place is kept in this browser.
  • Print the short summary sheet: the rules and key numbers, light on toner.
