Free millwright simulators

Drive the same simulators used in CrewForge lessons and videos. Every number is worked out live and drawn as a picture, so you can see what the maths means on the job. Push the rigging ones past their limits and they fail, the way they would on the floor.

Rigging, hoisting and moving loads

RIGGING

Slings: hitches and sling angles

Vertical, choker, basket and bridles in wire rope, chain or web. Lower the sling angle and watch the tension in each leg climb past the rating.

RIGGING

Load weight and centre of gravity

Weigh a plate, bar, pipe or skid from its size, find the centre of gravity, and see which leg or hoist carries more.

Open the simulator →
RIGGING

Wire rope inspection

Run a gloved hand along the rope, chalk the worst lay, count the broken wires and make the call: keep it or take it out of service.

Open the simulator →
RIGGING

Block and tackle

Reeve 1 to 8 parts of line, add sheave friction and pull. See your pull, how far you pull, and the load on the anchor.

Open the simulator →
HOISTING

Chain falls

A geared hand chain hoist next to a differential block. Compare the pull, the hand chain you pull and the efficiency.

Open the simulator →
HOISTING

Hand chain hoist

Pick the hoist and the load, hold to pull the hand chain and watch your pull, the ratio and the hook rise.

Open the simulator →
HOISTING

Overhead crane

Run a bridge crane from the pendant: hoist, trolley and bridge. Start and stop hard and watch the load swing.

Open the simulator →
CRANES

Mobile crane: operate it

Swing, boom, telescope and hoist to place the load. Read the load chart as you go, and let the load moment indicator stop an overload.

Open the simulator →
MOVING LOADS

Jacking one end

Move the centre of gravity, pick a bottle jack and pump. See how much of the machine the jack really carries.

Open the simulator →
MOVING LOADS

Skidding and rolling

Dry steel, grease, PTFE pads, rollers or skates, on the level or a slope. Compare the pull and see why a control line matters.

Open the simulator →

Hydraulics, alignment and pumps

HYDRAULICS

Hydraulic cylinder circuit

A pump, relief valve and 3-position directional control valve driving a double-acting cylinder. See the pressure the load needs, rod speed from pump flow, and what happens when the load needs more than the relief setting.

Open the simulator →
ALIGNMENT

Rim-and-face shaft alignment

Take dial readings at 12, 3, 6 and 9, check they're valid, then work out the shims and moves at the motor's front and back feet.

Open the simulator →
PUMPS

Pump curves and affinity laws

Change speed, impeller diameter, valve opening and static head. Watch the operating point move along the pump and system curves.

Open the simulator →
