Free millwright simulators

Drive the same simulators used in CrewForge lessons and videos. Every number is worked out live and shown step by step, in imperial or metric. Each one has guided challenges, predict-then-reveal questions and a common-mistakes mode.

RIGGING

Sling angles and load angle factors

Two-, three- and four-leg bridles, chokers and baskets. Lower the sling angle and watch the tension in each leg climb.

Open the simulator →
HYDRAULICS

Hydraulic cylinder circuit

A pump, relief valve and 3-position directional control valve driving a double-acting cylinder. See the pressure the load needs, rod speed from pump flow, and what happens when the load needs more than the relief setting.

Open the simulator →
ALIGNMENT

Rim-and-face shaft alignment

Take dial readings at 12, 3, 6 and 9, check they're valid, then work out the shims and moves at the motor's front and back feet.

Open the simulator →
RIGGING

Block and tackle and chain falls

Reeve 1 to 8 parts of line, add sheave friction, or switch to a chain fall. See your pull, how far you pull, and where the effort goes.

Open the simulator →
PUMPS

Pump curves and affinity laws

Change speed, impeller diameter, valve opening and static head. Watch the operating point move along the pump and system curves.
