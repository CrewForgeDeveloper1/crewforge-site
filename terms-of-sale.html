Early access is a pre-sale

CrewForge is still being built. When you buy early access you get every lesson that's already built the day you join, and new lessons as they're finished. Lesson order and release dates can change. We plan Level 1 for Winter 2027 and Levels 2 to 4 through 2027.

Our promise: if Level 1 isn't complete (every Level 1 lesson released) by March 31, 2027, you can ask for a full refund of your early-access payment until April 30, 2027. Email us from the address on your CrewForge Learn account; we refund to the original payment method within 30 days.

Price, tax and payment

  • Prices are in Canadian dollars. Prices are plus GST/HST for your province, added at checkout. If that changes, any GST/HST or provincial sales tax that applies is calculated and charged at the time of sale and shown before you pay.
  • Payment is taken in full at checkout, through PayPal. You can pay with a PayPal account or, without one, with a credit card, Visa Debit or Debit Mastercard. We never see or store your card number.
  • Your receipt comes from PayPal by email. Your enrolment shows in your CrewForge Learn account.

Your account

  • You need a CrewForge Learn account with a confirmed email address. One account is for one person: don't share your sign-in. Employers who want seats for a crew should contact us.
  • Access runs to the end date shown above. We email you 30 days and 7 days before your access ends.
  • Lessons work in an up-to-date web browser on a computer, tablet or phone.

Upgrades

If you have Level 1 early access, you can move up to the Full Program at a members' price, offered inside your Level 1 course. Upgrades replace refunds: your Level 1 payment is not refunded when you upgrade.

Extensions and grace period

  • Need more time? Extend your access by 3 months for $29 or 6 months for $49 (CAD).
  • If you've finished at least 75 % of a level when your access ends, you can ask once for a free 30-day grace period for that level.

When your access ends

You keep your CrewForge certificate of completion (and its verification code), your grade record and any textbook chapters you downloaded. Lessons, simulators in the course, question banks and exams close.

Certificates

A level is complete when you've passed (or challenged) every lesson and scored at least 70 % on the level exam. You then get a CrewForge certificate of completion. It recognises a private study program. It is not a Red Seal endorsement, a Certificate of Qualification or a provincial credential, it doesn't count toward apprenticeship hours, and we don't guarantee that you will pass any exam.

CrewForge courses are not offered or sold to residents of New Brunswick. If you buy from New Brunswick by mistake, we'll cancel the enrolment and refund you in full.
