Lockout and hazardous energy

Take a running hydraulic press to locked out and verified. Find the electrical, hydraulic, air, gravity and spring energy, bleed the accumulator, block the platen, then prove it is dead. See what happens when a gauge is never checked or nobody does a try-out.

The sample lesson. Every CrewForge lesson works like this one: short simulator pages, one idea each, with the narrator, practice questions and a summary sheet.

Open full screen · 7 pages · 8 practice questions · narrated (AI voice) · exam preparation only: always check your company's lockout procedure and your provincial OHS regulation.

What's in the lesson

  1. Find the energy. A small hydraulic press in a maintenance shop, running.
  2. Shut down and isolate. The same press, running.
  3. Lock and tag. The press is stopped and isolated: PRESS 1 disconnect OFF, air valve closed, hydraulic valve closed.
  4. Release stored energy. Locked out: three locks, three tags.
  5. Verify: test and try-out. Locked out, and every stored source released: gauge at zero, platen blocked, spring free.
  6. Group lockout. A bigger job: four workers on the press.
  7. Putting it together. The die on PRESS 1 needs changing.

How it works

  • One idea per page, each built around a simulator you work yourself. The last page puts it all together on one job.
  • A narrator reads each step aloud. Mute it or slow it down at the top right, or read it yourself.
  • Each step tells you what to do and what to watch. Stuck? Hint, then Show me: the simulator does it, then hands back.
  • Practice questions along the way. Pick a wrong answer and you see why it's wrong, then get a new, similar question.
  • After the steps, free play: every control unlocked, Reset, and "Give me a challenge".
  • Nothing locks. Back and Next always work, and your place is kept in this browser.
  • Print the short summary sheet: the rules and key numbers, light on toner.
